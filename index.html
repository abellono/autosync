Auto Sync for Fitbit

Weight, body fat, and sleep — with stages — from your Fitbit into Apple Health. On its own, every day.

AutomaticSyncs in the background a few times a day, and every time you open it. No buttons to remember.
Sleep with stagesLight, deep, REM and awake — the full picture, not just a total.
Your whole historyThe first sync imports everything your Fitbit account holds, years back.
Nothing collectedNo account, no server, no tracking. Your data goes from Google's Fitbit API straight into Apple Health, on your phone.
FreeOne job, done properly.

Works with any Fitbit tracker or Aria scale. Fitbit data is served by the Google Health API — the supported home of Fitbit data.
